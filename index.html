About us

A family-run class where every child is seen.

Accord Coaching Classes is a family-run coaching class in Vasai West for children from Jr KG to 10th standard.

Harshit Patel teaches Maths and Science, and his mother teaches all other subjects, with a special love for languages. We focus on personal attention and strong basics, so children understand what they learn and grow in confidence.
